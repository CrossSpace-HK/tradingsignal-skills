# Cross-Market Leaders

Use this workflow to find the strongest instruments across requested markets without pretending that unlike cohort scores share one absolute scale.

## Candidate criteria

- Use `strong` and `fresh_strength` to identify established and newly aligned leaders.
- Check `breakout` for a fresh continuation trigger and `near_cost` for a defensible pullback entry rather than a chase.
- Compare 1d, 4h, and 1h roles where the market supports them. Prefer aligned continuation, but do not reject a controlled lower-timeframe pause automatically.
- Rank leadership only within each market cohort. Compare candidates across markets using structure, trigger quality, payoff, freshness, and evidence completeness—not raw percentile values.

## Decision

Return at most three leaders that still offer a defensible invalidation and enough room to the next target. Separate `regime`, `trigger`, and `payoff` for every candidate. Downgrade an extended leader to `WATCH` when the trend is strong but the entry is poor.

Attach the corresponding trend, structural-method, and levels charts to the reasons they support.
