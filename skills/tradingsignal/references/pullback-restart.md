# Pullback Restart

Use this workflow for continuation setups in which the middle timeframe is temporarily moving against the higher-timeframe trend.

## Long structure

Require all three roles:

1. 1d trend and major structure remain bullish;
2. 4h pullback is controlled and has not broken the higher-timeframe thesis;
3. 1h turns back up with a fresh structural or momentum trigger.

Mirror the logic for shorts. Do not average the three timeframes into a generic conflict score.

## Validation

- Use granular structure methods to decide whether the 4h move is a pullback or structural failure.
- Use 1h only for timing; it cannot override a broken 1d thesis.
- Require an entry zone or trigger, a structural invalidation, and adequate room to the next resistance or support.
- Without the lower-timeframe restart, return `WATCH` and state the exact upgrade condition.

Bind each timeframe-specific reason to its own chart. Make the invalidation chart visually auditable.
